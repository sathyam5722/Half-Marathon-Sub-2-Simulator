How it works: each week you gain 5 seconds per mile × a runs factor × a speed factor × 0.93 to the power of the week number, so gains shrink over time. Runs factor: 2→0.6, 3→1.0, 4→1.3, 5→1.5, 6→1.45 (fatigue). Speed factor: 0→1.0, 1→1.25, 2→1.4, 3→1.45. Injury load = runs + 1.5 × speed workouts (low up to 5, moderate up to 7, high above 7). This is a planning aid, not medical or coaching advice.
